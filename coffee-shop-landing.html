Our Story

A small roaster with a big obsession for the perfect cup

Earl Brew & Cafe started in 2026 as a single drum roaster tucked behind a hardware store, run by two friends who couldn't agree on anything except that most coffee wasn't good enough. A decade later, that same stubborn standard shapes every bag we roast and every cup we pour.

We source direct from smallholder farms, roast in small batches every week, and train every barista by hand — because we believe coffee is at its best when it's made slowly, by people who care.
