Restaurant Story

From one oven to a neighborhood staple.

Pizzeria 90 opened in a narrow storefront with one wood oven and a recipe box carried from Naples. Word spread fast — by year two, the line stretched down the block.

2006Pizzeria 90 opens its doors with a single brick oven.
2011Expanded seating to meet growing weekend demand.
2016Launched delivery across the whole neighborhood.
2021Won Best Neapolitan Pizza, three years running.
2026Serving 50,000+ pizzas and counting.
